# Approach check

Read by the manager skill at a phase's third fix round, and when the same-blockers brake trips
with fix rounds left (**Brakes**).

Ask whether the **approach** is wrong rather than the last attempt: (1) same approach with a
sharper brief, or (2) a materially different approach inside the approved design — each at most
once per phase, within the model strategy (see "Not enough model"); (3) a design change is an
owner decision.

Environment, permission or tooling failures (network, auth, disk) are not evidence against the
approach: repair once or report; never abandon a sound approach over them.
